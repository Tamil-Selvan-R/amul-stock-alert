Product Information

First time developed in India, Amul lactose free chocolate whey protein provides 25g whey protein (5g BCAA) in a single serve sachet of 34g.

Make Amul whey protein a part of your and your family’s daily diet whether you are at home or on-the-go. The convenient packaging will allow you to even carry your protein in your pocket wherever you go.

  • 5g BCAA
  • Lactose free
  • Chocolate

Empty the Amul whey protein sachet in an Amul shaker and add 200-300ml water or milk as per your preference.

Shake well and enjoy delicious dose of lactose free whey protein.
